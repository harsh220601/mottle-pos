Mottle AI

Answers from your books.
Kept on your iPad.

Ask in plain English. Mottle AI looks up the answer in your own records and shows it as a chart, a table or a checklist — all without an internet connection.

Built to be right, not just fluent.

THE APP DOES THE MATH

Numbers you can audit

Revenue, occupancy, refunds and balances are calculated by Mottle POS from your ledger. The language model only explains them.

MEANINGFUL COLOR

Green means good

Revenue up shows green, refunds up shows red, tax is neutral. Every change also carries an arrow and a label, so color is never the only signal.

YOU CONFIRM

Nothing happens without you

Mottle AI can prepare a booking, but the reservation is created only after an employee confirms the complete quote. It never takes payments or issues refunds, and drafts are yours to review and send.

Questions to try

“How did we do this month vs last?”

A side-by-side comparison with the difference and percentage change.

“Which rooms are dirty?”

Room status counts and a breakdown chart.

“Any refunds in the last 7 days?”

Refund totals, flagged when they rise.

“Who’s arriving today?”

Today’s arrivals, or a clear “No arrivals” when the list is empty.

“Is night audit done?”

The latest audit’s status as a checklist.

“Book room 12 for two nights”

A complete quote to check — the reservation is made only when you confirm it.

Privacy

No guest data is sent to an AI service.

The model is downloaded once and runs on the device’s own chip. Your questions, your guests and your numbers stay on your iPad. You can clear Mottle AI history at any time in Settings.

Mottle AI requires a supported iPad or iPhone and enough free storage for the model. Responses can be wrong — review anything important before acting on it.
