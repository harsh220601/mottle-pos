Questions

What happens when the 30 days end?

A Mottle POS Pro subscription is required to make business changes — new reservations, check-ins, payments and edits. Your existing records stay on the device and remain viewable.

How do I subscribe or restore a purchase?

In the app, go to Manage → Settings → Plan & Rooms. Subscribe there, or tap Restore Purchases if you already subscribed with the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal in your Apple Account subscription settings. How to cancel a subscription.

Is the price the same everywhere?

$35 per month is the US price. The App Store shows the price in your local currency before you confirm.

Does Mottle POS process card payments?

No. Mottle POS records payments you take — cash, card or other methods — but it is not a payment processor and never handles card numbers.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Use is governed by the Apple Standard EULA and our Terms of Use; see our Privacy Policy.
