Data deletion

Delete your data.

Because your records live on your device, you can remove them yourself — no request to us required.

Last updated October 3, 2026

Clear Mottle AI history

Open Mottle AI, then use the option in Settings to clear its history. This removes saved questions and answers from the device.

Delete individual records

Records can be edited or removed in the app where your permissions allow. Some financial history is kept in the audit log by design so your books stay consistent; deleting the app removes it.

Delete backups

Encrypted backups are files you saved — for example in Files or on a computer. Delete those files wherever you stored them.

Delete everything

  1. Export a backup first if you might need your records later.
  2. On the iPad or iPhone, touch and hold the Mottle POS (HOTEL POS) icon.
  3. Tap Remove App, then Delete App.

Deleting the app removes all of its data stored on that device. Repeat on each device you use.

Cancel your subscription

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Cancel Mottle POS Pro in your Apple Account subscription settings. See Apple’s instructions.

Support form submissions

If you sent us a message through the support form and want it deleted, submit a new request through the same form asking for deletion, and we will remove it.
