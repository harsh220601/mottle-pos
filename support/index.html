Quick checks

Mottle AI says the model is unavailable

Mottle AI needs a supported device, enough free storage and a one-time model download. Connect to Wi-Fi, open Mottle AI and follow the download prompt. If storage is low, free some space and try again.

A report shows numbers I didn’t expect

Reports follow the business day, which closes when night audit is finalized. Check that the previous night audit is complete and that the date range is the one you meant. Payments and refunds count on the day they were recorded.

My subscription isn’t showing in the app

Open Manage → Settings → Plan & Rooms and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple Account you subscribed with and is online for a moment.

I need to move to a new iPad

Create an encrypted backup in Settings, move the file to the new device, and restore it there with your backup password. Mottle POS can’t recover a forgotten backup password.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed in your Apple Account. See Apple’s instructions.
